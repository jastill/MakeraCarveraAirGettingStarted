# Safety Guidelines - Carvera Air CNC Machine

## ⚠️ READ THIS BEFORE OPERATING YOUR MACHINE

### Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

**REQUIRED for every operation:**
- Safety glasses (ANSI Z87.1 rated)
- Close-toed shoes
- Long pants (no shorts)

**RECOMMENDED:**
- Hearing protection for extended use
- Dust mask when cutting materials that produce fine particles
- Shop apron to protect clothing

### Pre-Operation Safety Check

Complete this checklist before every use:

#### Machine Inspection
- [ ] Emergency stop button is functional and accessible
- [ ] All guards and enclosures are in place
- [ ] Spindle rotates freely by hand (when powered off)
- [ ] No loose tools or debris in work area
- [ ] All axes move smoothly without binding

#### Workholding Verification
- [ ] Material is securely clamped or held
- [ ] No clamps interfere with tool paths
- [ ] Material cannot shift during cutting
- [ ] Adequate clearance around cutting area

#### Tool and Setup Check
- [ ] Cutting tool is sharp and undamaged
- [ ] Tool is properly secured in holder
- [ ] Tool length and diameter are correctly set
- [ ] Spindle speed and feed rates are appropriate for material

### During Operation Safety Rules

#### NEVER
- Leave the machine unattended while cutting
- Reach into the cutting area while spindle is running
- Open enclosures during operation
- Wear loose clothing, jewelry, or gloves
- Operate without safety glasses
- Force the emergency stop unless there's a genuine emergency

#### ALWAYS
- Monitor the cutting process
- Keep workspace clean and well-lit
- Maintain clear access to emergency stop
- Listen for unusual sounds or vibrations
- Stop immediately if anything seems wrong

### Material-Specific Safety

#### Wood and Composites
- Risk of splinters and dust inhalation
- Fire hazard from fine chips
- Use dust collection when available
- Keep fire extinguisher nearby

#### Plastics
- Risk of toxic fumes from some materials
- Melting can create poor surface finish
- Ensure adequate ventilation
- Know your material's melting point

#### Metals (Aluminum, etc.)
- Sharp chips can cause cuts
- Hot chips present burn risk
- Use coolant or air blast when possible
- Wear eye protection even when observing

### Emergency Procedures

#### If Something Goes Wrong
1. **Press emergency stop immediately**
2. Turn off spindle using software
3. Do not touch moving parts until fully stopped
4. Assess the situation safely
5. Contact support if needed

#### Common Emergency Situations
- **Tool breakage**: Stop immediately, clear broken pieces safely
- **Workpiece comes loose**: Stop, secure properly before continuing
- **Unusual noises**: Stop and inspect before proceeding
- **Sparks or burning smell**: Stop immediately, check for problems

### Tool Safety

#### Handling Cutting Tools
- Always handle by the shank, never by cutting edges
- Store in protective containers or blocks
- Inspect before each use for damage
- Replace worn or damaged tools immediately

#### Tool Installation
- Clean tool holder and tool shank before installation
- Use proper torque specifications for tool holders
- Verify tool is fully seated and secure
- Test tool runout if precision is critical

### Electrical Safety

- Keep liquids away from electrical components
- Do not operate with wet hands
- Ensure proper grounding
- Report damaged cords or connections immediately
- Use appropriate surge protection

### Maintenance Safety

- Always disconnect power before maintenance
- Follow lockout/tagout procedures if applicable
- Use only manufacturer-approved parts
- Keep maintenance records
- Have qualified personnel perform major repairs

### Training Requirements

**Before operating the Carvera Air, you must:**
- Read and understand this safety guide
- Complete the basic training examples
- Demonstrate safe setup and operation procedures
- Know the location and operation of all safety devices
